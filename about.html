What it does
🌧
Live Vancouver weather
Real-time temperature, feels like, rain chance, UV index, sunrise & sunset, hourly forecast, and a smart trip advisory.
🚇
Transit status
Live status for Expo Line, Canada Line, Millennium Line, B-Lines, SeaBus, West Coast Express and more.
🎟
Vancouver events
Real events — Canucks games, concerts, theatre — plus curated local events like farmers markets, free museum days, and community happenings.
🗺
Route planner
Type any two locations and get live transit directions on a map with step-by-step instructions and a delay risk prediction.
🗓
Trip planner calendar
Plan a full day out. Add multiple stops, get suggested transit routes between each one, set reminders, and save your plan.
🔗
Share your trip
Generate a link to share your full trip plan with friends — they open it and see your exact stops, routes and notes.
🏙
Vancouver vibe suggestions
Based on today's weather, the app suggests real Vancouver spots — rainy day? It recommends the VPL, Kafka's Coffee, or the VAG. Sunny? Kits Beach and the Seawall.
⏱
TransLink buffer
Unlike Google, we know Vancouver transit. The app automatically adds buffer time during rush hour, rain, major events, and at busy hubs like Commercial-Broadway.
🌿
Hyper-local events
Free farmers markets, UBC museum Tuesdays, Lynn Canyon, people-watching at Granville Island — things Google won't show you but Vancouverites know and love.
✨
Generate my trip
Pick a time window, starting location and vibe — the app builds your full Vancouver day automatically, weather-aware and transit-timed.
📊
Plan confidence score
Every generated plan gets a confidence score based on weather, rush hour and events — so you know how reliable your day is before you leave.
How to use it
1
Check the dashboard
Open the app to see live Vancouver weather, all transit lines status, and what events are happening today — including free local community events most apps miss.
2
Plan your route
Use the Route Planner at the bottom — type where you're starting and where you're going. The map draws your transit route instantly with step-by-step directions.
3
Check your vibe suggestions
Based on today's weather the app suggests real Vancouver spots. Rainy day? It recommends indoor spots like the Vancouver Public Library or VAG. Rare sunny day? Kits Beach and the Seawall.
4
Open the Trip Planner
Click the Trip Planner button in the header. Pick a date on the calendar — you'll see the weather forecast and events for that specific day.
5
Add your stops
Type each stop of your day — From, To, leave time, and the app automatically suggests the best bus or SkyTrain route and fills in your arrive time.
6
See it on the map
Click "Show on map" to see all your stops as numbered pins with routes drawn between them in different colors, and full directions for each leg.
7
Save & share
Save your plan so it shows on the calendar with a green dot. Share it with friends via a link — they'll see your exact trip when they open it.
8
Or just generate your whole day
Don't want to plan manually? Hit "Generate my trip" — pick your vibe (outdoor, culture, food, chill) and available hours, and the app builds your full Vancouver itinerary automatically with transit directions and a confidence score.
